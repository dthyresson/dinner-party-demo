# Dietary and kitchen rules

- Vegan: no meat, fish, dairy, eggs or honey.
- Vegetarian: no meat or fish (dairy may be acceptable, subject to preferences).
- Gluten-free: verify ingredients, packaged labels and cross-contact before asserting suitable.
- Severe shellfish allergy: eliminate shellfish from the entire menu; verify sauces/stock, labels, utensils and surface-cleaning plans. Do not offer a merely 'shellfish-free plate' beside shrimp preparation.
- Any unspecified risk or label uncertainty must be surfaced rather than guessed away.

# Chef notes

The recipe catalogue in `recipes.json` is a list of options, not a chosen menu.

- No menu has been selected. Confirm headcount, dietary needs and timing with the Guest Manager and Scheduler before proposing courses.
- Confirm the guest count before finalizing portions; quantities are per each recipe's `serves` value and must be rescaled.
- Treat every dish as a proposal until ingredients, packaged labels and cross-contact safeguards are verified. A "shellfish-free ingredients" tag is not a certified allergen-free claim.
- Any dish containing shellfish needs explicit safety sign-off before it is offered.

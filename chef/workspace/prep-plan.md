# Prep plan — Thanksgiving dinner, 12 covers, 2:00 PM service

Thursday 26 November 2026, 2:00 PM. Venue: the sister's house in Andover.
**Two ovens, confirmed by DT.** Turkey: 16 lb assumed, 325F, 3 3/4-4 1/4 hours, 10 minutes standing. Three sides need 350F and 115-120 minutes between them. Ham and mash need no oven.

**The hob is now the tight resource, not the oven.** Between 1:35 and 1:55 three jobs queue on it: onions flambé (20 min), carrots (5-10), and the mash finish (10). Two rings cannot do all three without something sitting. The mash is the one that suffers most from waiting — it goes gluey — so if there are only two rings, onions go on first, mash finishes while the onions rest off the heat, and carrots take the last free ring. If there is one ring, the mash has to be held in a warm bowl and finished in stages, and I'd want to know that before Thursday.

**One dish still has to wait in the oven.** Oven B carries 115-120 minutes of sides and they can't all finish at 2:00. Green bean casserole goes last so its onions are crisp, which leaves it about 35 minutes out of the oven. No recipe here gives a holding method, so that's a known compromise rather than a solved problem.

**The ham wants watching, not timing.** No oven step, but "bring to room temperature" has no duration in what I was given. It should come out early and be checked rather than scheduled — a ham still cold in the middle at 2:00 is the one failure mode this dish has.

## Flags

- **The bird's weight is my assumption.** 16 lb, in the 14-18 lb band. If DT changes it, steps 4, 14 and 17 move, and the whole morning shifts.
- **The times assume an unstuffed bird.** Stuffing in the cavity insulates and lengthens the roast by an amount no recipe here states. If the bird goes in stuffed, step 4 starts earlier by a margin I can't put a number on.
- **No stuffing temperature is supplied.** The recipe's 165F covers breast and thigh. Until the stuffing's own safe temperature exists, I won't sign off the in-bird batch.
- **The stuffing card has no method, temperature, time or pan**, so batch B is unsequenceable.
- **The mash direction has no salt, no boil time and no yield.** I've recorded it exactly as given. Five pounds of russets is a lot of mash beside a 16 lb bird and a sweet potato casserole, and I can't say what it serves.
- **Marshmallows normally contain gelatin** — the sweet potato dish isn't vegetarian unless the pack says so. The mash is vegetarian but not vegan: butter and cream.
- **Mustard is a declared allergen** and honey mustard may carry egg. Two jars, two labels.
- **Pan size for 1.5x sweet potato casserole** is unresolved; the recipe's 9x13 is sized for 8 servings.
- **Thawing.** A 16 lb bird, if frozen, needs days. Two days out, and the recipe is silent on fresh versus frozen.
- Raw bird juices cross-contact every surface at a venue I have not seen.
- Freezer space for the pie is unverified; five hours of freezing fails if that freezer is full.
- Flambé in someone else's kitchen: open flame, unknown extraction, unknown surface.
- Corn Pudding has no stated yield, so it may not carry 12.

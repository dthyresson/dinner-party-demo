# Organization agent instructions

This is a fictional simulation. The scenario clock is **2026-11-23**, independent of real-world time.

1. Read your role's `AGENTS.md`, `ROLE.md`, and `README.md` before acting.
2. Perform only work covered by your role's authority and assigned resources.
3. Ask other roles for missing information. Share the smallest actionable amount of information needed.
4. Do not silently assume a response, spending approval, RSVP, or guest permission.
5. State estimates and uncertainty explicitly. Check compatibility of quantities, prices, dietary restrictions, timing, and headcount.
6. Send material status changes and blockers to the Chief of Staff.
7. Save outputs under role `workspace/` or shared `results/`; use local messages for simulated communications.
8. Never use real accounts, personal information, outside web sites, or network access. All identities, prices, and records are fictional.
9. Do not read `fixtures/` or `EVALUATION.md` while acting as an agent; those belong to the presenter/evaluator.

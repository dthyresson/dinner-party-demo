# Organization agent instructions

This is a fictional simulation. The scenario clock is **2026-11-23**, independent of real-world time.

1. Read your role's `AGENTS.md`, `ROLE.md` and `README.md`, then your event's `brief.md`, before acting.
2. Perform only work covered by your role's authority. Another role's work is not yours to do, even when you could.
3. You are one session among several. Your folder is the only one you may write, and you never read another role's `data/` or `workspace/`. You ask the session that owns the fact.
4. Ask the owning role for missing information. Share the smallest actionable amount; never dump a roster, a catalogue or a ledger into the room.
5. Do not silently assume a response, spending approval, RSVP, or guest permission.
6. State estimates and uncertainty explicitly. Check compatibility of quantities, prices, dietary restrictions, timing, and headcount.
7. Send material status changes and blockers to the Planner.
8. Roles are positional. You do not name yourself or another agent. A display name in the event manifest labels that event's room only and changes nothing about your role or authority.
9. On your first turn, join the event room, introduce yourself, then wait for work addressed to you.
10. Write only the files your role lists, and create no others. Keep tasks in `workspace/tasks.json`. Publish only the one file in `results/` your role owns; every deliverable has exactly one writer.
11. Never invent a fact, price, guest, time, quantity or approval. If it was not given to you by the brief or the owning role, record it as `unknown` and ask.
12. Never use real accounts, personal information, outside web sites, or network access. All identities, prices, and records are fictional.
13. Do not read `fixtures/` while acting as an agent; those belong to the presenter.

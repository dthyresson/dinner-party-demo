# Dietary handling

- Relay a constraint, not a person: "one guest requires a fully vegan meal" is enough for the Chef.
- Send allergy and cross-contact constraints first and in full; they are safety facts, not
  preferences.
- Keep the roster, contact details and unrelated preferences out of room messages.
- A dietary need does not choose a dish. The Chef decides the dish; you supply the constraint.
- A request to bring another guest is a request. It is not a confirmed headcount change until the
  host approves it.
- Drafts only: nothing is invited, accepted or sent by this role.
